Hover Preview

Resting the mouse on a card pops out a larger card with a muted preview clip. Changes apply after reloading the web page.

General

When off, cards show the still image and details only.
How long the pointer rests on a card before it pops out. Netflix uses about 500.

Where

Clip selection (spoiler guard)

Shows always preview their first two episodes. Clips never use intros, recaps, credits or the last 15% of a video.

Streaming

Browser-compatible files (MP4/H.264/AAC) stream untouched; everything else is transcoded to a small preview.
Media Bar Card plays the same clips in its home banner. The banner is much bigger than a card, so its transcode is sharper.
